How it works

Three simple steps.

The intended process keeps submission simple and gives the administration a clear way to review feedback.

1

Submit

Write your message or record a voice note and choose the relevant category.

2

Review

Authorized administrators receive submissions through a protected dashboard.

3

Take action

Staff can categorize concerns, follow up where appropriate and update the resolution status.

Please note: This channel is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact appropriate campus officials or emergency services directly. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through this website.
